# Click Note Taker Privacy Policy - Draft

This document is a product draft and must be reviewed, completed, and published at a stable public URL before external distribution.

## Information stored by the app

Click Note Taker can store worker profile details, client profile details, support-session notes, timestamps, report fields, and voice recordings entered by the user.

## Where information is stored

App records are stored locally on the user's device. Click Note Taker does not currently provide a cloud account or upload app records to a Click Note Taker server.

## Device permissions

The app requests microphone access only when voice recording is used. It can use device biometrics to unlock the app when the user enables that option.

## Sharing information

The user can copy or share a generated report through other apps installed on the device. The receiving app or service has its own privacy practices, and the user is responsible for selecting an authorised destination.

## Data retention and deletion

Users can delete individual notes and client profiles in the app. Deleting a client also deletes that client's notes from the app database. A complete erase-all-data workflow must be implemented and documented before public release.

## Security

The app provides PIN and optional biometric access controls. Local SQLite records are not currently described as database-level encrypted. Users must secure their devices and follow applicable workplace, privacy, and record-keeping requirements.

## Contact

Before publication, add the legal entity responsible for the app, a privacy contact email, a postal address if required, the effective date, and the applicable jurisdiction.
